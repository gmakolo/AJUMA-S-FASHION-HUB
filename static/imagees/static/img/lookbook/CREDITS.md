# Sample photographs

These six photographs stand in for the shop's own work so an empty catalogue
still looks like a shop. They are freely licensed pictures from Wikimedia
Commons, resized and re-encoded by this project's own image pipeline. **Replace
them with your own photographs before using this as a real storefront** — delete
the sample dresses in the studio and the files go with them.

Each licence requires that the photographer is credited and the licence named.
Resizing does not change either requirement, and a copy stays under the same
licence.

`01.jpg` is achi, the handwoven cloth of the Igala people of Kogi State,
photographed folded rather than worn. The garments in the rest are by their
designers — Clan (Teni, Aba and Tiwa Sagoe), Jewel by Lisa, Amina Agueznay,
Mouna Belgrini — and the names, prices and descriptions in `seed.go` are
invented sample copy, not those designers' products.
